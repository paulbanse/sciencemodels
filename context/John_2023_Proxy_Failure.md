# Dead Rats, Dopamine, Performance Metrics, and Peacock Tails

**Yohan J. John, Leigh Caldwell, Dakota E. McCoy, Oliver Braganza**  
*Behavioral and Brain Sciences*, forthcoming (online 2023). DOI: 10.1017/S0140525X23002753

## Core Claim

A wide range of phenomena — **Goodhart's law, Campbell's law, the Cobra effect, the McNamara fallacy, goal displacement, Fisherian runaway, addiction, grade inflation, KPI corruption** — share a common structure that the authors call **proxy failure**. Whenever a regulator uses an imperfect proxy measure to incentivise or select agents, **pressure arises that pushes the proxy away from the goal**. This is an inherent risk of goal-oriented systems, not a contingent malfunction.

## Central Framework

### Core components

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Regulator | Sets the proxy and incentive structure. |
| Agent | Produces outputs that the proxy measures. |
| Proxy $P$ | Observable, used to regulate agents. |
| Goal $G$ | Unobservable (or costly to observe) objective. |
| Hacking / Gaming | Agent effort directed at $P$ rather than $G$. |

### Three informational limits necessitating proxies (Section 3.2)

1. **Legibility limit**: Regulator cannot fully specify the goal → proxies coordinate agents.
2. **Prediction limit**: Cannot foresee all consequences of agent actions → proxies are necessary heuristics.
3. **Choice limit**: Infinite possible actions, single scalar metric → proxies collapse dimensionality.

## Domain Applications

### 4.1 Neuroscience: Dopamine as a proxy for value

- Dopamine signals are a **neural proxy** for "value"; addictive drugs **hack** this proxy.
- The disconnect between "revealed" and "normative" preferences is cast as **within-person proxy failure**.
- Constraint: conscious control, legal/social norms, monitoring.

### 4.2 Economics: KPIs and performance metrics

- Extensively documented proxy failure in management and economics literature (Kerr 1975; Holmström 2017; Baker 2002).
- **Principal-agent models** show: weaker proxies imply lower optimal incentive strength; the prevalence of fixed wages is explained by proxy failure risk.
- **Hierarchical KPIs**: parochial KPIs at department level diverge from firm-level goals (Tardieu et al. 2020).
- **Market selection as constraint**: extreme proxy failure → bankruptcy; market constrains divergence.

### 4.3 Ecology: Sexual selection and signalling

- Peacock tails as **runaway proxies** for genetic quality; Fisherian runaway is a **special case** of the proxy treadmill.
- Constraints: natural selection (survival cost of excessive ornaments).
- Proxy failure offers a novel lens for **evolutionary "decadence"** (Prum 2017).

## Three Consequences of Proxy Failure

### 5.1 The Proxy Treadmill

An arms race between agent (hacking) and regulator (counter-hacking), yielding:

- **Instability**: no stable signalling equilibria.
- **Escalation**: quantitative inflation of signals (peacock tails, grade inflation, publication counts).
- **Elaboration**: qualitative modification of proxies (new criteria, complexification).

### 5.2 The Proxy Cascade

In **nested hierarchical systems**, a higher-level proxy constrains lower-level proxy failure. Market selection at the top cascades down to constrain department-level KPI gaming.

### 5.3 Proxy Appropriation

Higher-level systems **harness** proxy failure at lower levels to pursue their own goals — e.g., aspartame hacking sweetness receptors to serve conscious (or corporate) goals of sweetness without calories. Dark patterns, addictive marketing, and SEO appropriation are further examples.

## Positioning

| Field | Reception |
|-------|-----------|
| Metascience | Integrates "reproducibility crisis" with incentive economics. |
| Management / organisational theory | Unifies KPI failure, Campbell's law, Goodhart's law under one heading. |
| Evolutionary biology | Recasts Fisherian runaway as an instance of the proxy treadmill. |
| Neuroscience | Novel framing of addiction; links "revealed vs. normative preferences" to proxy logic. |
| Political science / public administration | Provides a unifying lens for the McNamara fallacy, the Cobra effect, and "tyranny of metrics". |

## Robustness & Caveats

- **Breadth vs. depth**: necessarily superficial in each domain; the paper invites extensions.
- **No formal model of the treadmill or cascade**: diagrams are conceptual, not mathematical.
- **Agency and intentionality**: the paper oscillates between intentional hacking and selection-based dynamics without fully resolving the tension.
- **Constraint mechanisms** (market, natural selection, conscious control) are posited but not endogenised.

## Relevance to the MA Thesis & Project

- **Theoretical backbone** of the proposal's analytical framework: collaboration with high-rank co-authors can be read as a proxy for research quality; if the proxy is gamed (rank homophily rather than intellectual fit), proxy failure arises.
- **Braganza's ESS model** (2020) is an early instance of this general framework.
- **Hunting note** (Kapeller, Paul, Braganza 2026) is a pre-formalisation companion.
- The cascade and appropriation concepts offer **testable conjectures** about how institutional hierarchies reproduce themselves in co-authorship networks.

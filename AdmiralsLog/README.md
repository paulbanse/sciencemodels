# AdmiralsLog

Records for this project: what was decided (`decisions/accepted/`), what was
ruled out (`decisions/rejected/`, `bugs/deadends/`), what was fixed
(`bugs/resolved/`), what is open (`bugs/` top level), what is parked
(`ideas/`, `unexplored/`, `contributions/`), what awaits discussion
(`proposals/`, with completed experimental proposals in `proposals/executed/`)
and its register, what happened (`logs/`), and what is retired
(`archive/`). The authoritative charter, including the handling rules, is
`../IDEA.md`.

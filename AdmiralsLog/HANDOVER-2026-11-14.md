# 1. Where we stand

## 1.1 The project in one paragraph

`sciencemodels` is a Mesa ABM of science on a toroidal epistemic landscape
(20x20): scientists harvest knowledge, earn a Cobb-Douglas reward mixing
novelty and *visibility*, accrue prestige, and supervisors pick advisees
proportional to prestige. The model prices prestige against epistemic ground
truth (ideal merit = the greedy-optimal harvest allocation) and asks: **when
does the prestige hierarchy stop tracking merit -- the proxy-failure issue**
(John 2023; Braganza 2020). The collaborator-choice channel was deliberately
bracketed until the quality-prestige interplay was understood; **Path B now
brings it in** (design menu W3-C, decisions pending). Phase-1 paper spine:
*Does Prestige Track Quality?* (`docs/way_forward.md`).

## 1.5 Literature and empirical anchor

46 web-verified studies in four clusters (`docs/related_publications.md`, v2.0):
epistemic-landscape and division-of-labor models (Kitcher; Weisberg and
Muldoon; Zollman; Hong and Page; Avin), metric gaming and proxy failure
(John et al.; Braganza; Smaldino and McElreath), collaborator choice, team
science and prestige (Merton; Guimera et al.; Wuchty et al.; Petersen et al.;
Wu et al.; Smith et al.; Bala and Goyal; Jackson and Rogers; Squazzoni), and
the project's own foundations (Dobusch and Kapeller and the
Kapeller--Paul--Braganza note, among others). **Niche claim:** no published model combines landscape search +
visibility-proxy incentives + partner selection scored against an ideal
allocation. Empirical anchor: the Klann coauthorship data
(`docs/connections_niklas.md`) -- prestige-merit wedge 0.50 (merit ~ reward)
vs 0.07 (merit ~ reward-rank), within-author own-prestige corr -0.257 vs
coauth_rank slope +0.004, Markov retention 0.3995/entry 0.0619, team size
2.65 (median 2), repeated-collab share 0.34, dyad-corrected O/E elite cells
3.1/3.0/2.3. These are a **documented option menu** for later calibration
(`docs/calibration_moments.yaml`), not targets.
